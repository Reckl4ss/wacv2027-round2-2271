# Scientific and reproducibility limits

- Findings are benchmark-bound observational associations. Sign reversal is
  not universal, and capacity association does not identify a causal mechanism.
- Orientation invariance of PCA variance does not establish invariance of signed
  loading direction. Outcome-informed orientation increases cross-axis agreement.
- True representation-dimension matching is unavailable: the cached count field
  is not a validated dimension, and its two supports do not overlap. Effective-rank
  common-range analyses are unmatched sensitivity checks.
- Matched sample size does not establish cohort representativeness. The size-axis
  intersection has mild shifts that do not survive within-axis multiplicity
  correction. The upstream 500-cohort selection algorithm is unavailable.
- Search screening uses cached target calibration labels. Named trap improvements
  do not establish average-case benefit or superiority to all fixed baselines.
- External audit inputs provide 13 fields against a required count of 21; the
  exact 21-name contract is not fully specified by the frozen material. Effective
  rank is absent. No audited population is eligible for the complete decomposition.
- SWAP/AZ-NAS results apply to the aligned populations and specified initializations.
  Negative-infinity AZ-NAS sentinels are retained and ranked as lowest ties.
- The recorded 2026-08-25 search did not locate official licensed implementations
  for L-SWAG/InTrain. This is a dated coverage limitation, not a negative result
  and not a claim about their current availability.
- The NTK proposition concerns a frozen at-initialization target and squared
  Pearson correlation. It does not prove results about trained accuracy.
- The package supports analysis from frozen measurements. It does not yet provide
  a fully validated end-to-end regeneration of every upstream proxy measurement.
  Large search traces and external benchmark inputs require separate provisioning.
